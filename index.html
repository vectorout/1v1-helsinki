Training

Time to work
on your game.

Private football training for players aged 6–18. We work on the skills you need in matches, with time to practise and feedback as you go. Sessions take place in Helsinki and Espoo.

01

1v1
skills

Practise body feints, changes of pace and taking the ball past a defender. We break the movements down, then put them together with the ball.

  • Body feints
  • Dribbling
  • Changes of pace
02

Speed &
movement

Work on your first steps and changes of direction. Practise how you move towards the ball, away from an opponent and into space.

  • First step
  • Acceleration
  • Direction changes
03

Touch &
footwork

Spend time on your first touch, close control and using both feet. Repeat the basics, then try them at a quicker pace.

  • First touch
  • Close control
  • Both feet

The Coach

Here's why I coach.

Injuries ended my time in academy football. I remember wanting more help with my own game. That's what led me to individual coaching.

Now I coach players aged 6–18 in Helsinki and Espoo. We work on dribbling, first touch, footwork and 1v1 situations. I'll explain what we're practising, give you feedback and help you work through it.
